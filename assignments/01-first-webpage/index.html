About Estonia

Estonia is a small country in Northern Europe. Its capital city is Tallinn. The population of Estonia is about 1.3 million people. The official language is Estonian.

Places to visit

Tallinn (capital city), Tartu (college town), Valga (southernmost city), Narva (city close to Russian border)

Fun facts

1. Skype was created by an Estonian-based development team.

2. Nearly half of Estonia is covered by green forests.

3. Can do almost everything online, from voting to starting a business in 15 minutes.
